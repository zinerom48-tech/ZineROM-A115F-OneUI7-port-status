## Failed approaches that must not be repeated

1. Generating another ZIP with unrelated changes before the `enablefilecrypto` root cause is identified.
2. Treating `wait_for_keymaster` breadcrumbs as a fix. They are diagnostic only.
3. Treating `K00R` or QSEE `0x72000206` as a confirmed root cause; both occur on booting devices.
4. Adding another Keymaster bridge or changing FBE/fstab without a raw vold/keystore2 log.
5. Disabling FBE or formatting `/data`, `/keydata`, or `/keyrefuge` as a blind workaround; this destroys data and does not prove the underlying Keymaster path works.
6. Assuming the donor system is 64-bit-only without checking; it declares 32-bit ABI support and ships 32-bit binaries. The remaining 32-bit risk is library-level, not architectural.
7. Treating a truncated ext4 listing as proof that a library is absent. The first `/system/lib64` result ended at `read beyond end of image`; the directory data was simply outside the decompressed prefix.
8. Flashing on a different model/bootloader or a device without a verified stock restore path.
9. Claiming “boot fixed” from static tests, CRC checks, or package validation alone.
